# What's new

Each release's notes on GitHub come from its section below.

## v1.4

**Update in one click.** When a new version is out, an **Update to …** button
appears at the top of the window. It shows what's new, then downloads,
checks and installs the update and restarts the app. Your settings and undo
history are kept. You can also choose **Later** or **Skip this version**, or
look for one yourself with **Check now** under More options.

**Rules by name.** Custom rules can now look at words in a file's name, not
just its type: send anything with "invoice" or "receipt" in the name to
Documents/Receipts, say, whatever kind of file it is. One-click ideas get you
started: Receipts, Statements, Travel, CVs, Contracts and Wallpapers.

**Archive old files.** Under More options, choose to move files you haven't
changed for 3 months, 6 months or a year into an Archive folder. They're
sorted the same way inside it, out of the way but never deleted.

**Tidies up after itself.** The app now clears away what it leaves on disk:
copies of itself left behind if it was force-closed (about 60 MB each on
Windows), downloaded updates, and undo history older than six months.

## v1.3

**File Organiser now runs on Mac.** Download `FileOrganiser-mac.dmg`, open it,
and drag the app into Applications. It needs a Mac with an Apple chip (M1 or
newer). The first time, macOS asks you to allow it in System Settings, Privacy
& Security, Open Anyway.

**A fresh look**

- A cleaner window in light or dark, with summary cards that show what a scan
  found at a glance.
- Pick your own accent colour: purple, blue, teal, green, orange or pink.
- A short welcome tour the first time you open the app. Press **? How it
  works** to see it again.
- Real tick boxes, plainer names (Map, Files to move, Tidy, Undo a tidy) and
  an explanation on every option when you rest the mouse on it.
- The settings most people don't need now sit behind **More options**.

**Handier**

- Double-click any file to open it. Right-click for Show in Explorer (or
  Finder), Open containing folder, and Skip or Include.
- Stop a scan part way with **Stop scan** or Esc.
- Keyboard shortcuts: Ctrl+O (⌘O) choose a folder, F5 scan, Ctrl+Enter tidy,
  Ctrl+Z undo, F1 welcome tour.
- The app tells you when a new version is out. You can turn this off under
  More options.

**Smaller things**

- Mac installers (`.pkg`, and `.dmg` on a Mac) count as old installers.
- Each download is now opened and given a test scan before it is published.

## v1.2

- The map: see your plan as an animated graph, like Obsidian's graph view.
- **Before / after** plays the tidy: files fly from the folder to where they go.
- Fold big folders with a double-click; size dots by disk space.
- Dark theme, and the map remembers its settings.
